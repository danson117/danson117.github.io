1. Scope — what TOT manages

In scope (authorization-impacting)

TOT manages customer procedure training and authorization-related requirements that directly affect engineer authorization. Not general HAECO training.

  • Customer procedure training
  • Authorization-related procedure training
  • Customer-specific mandatory training

Out of scope

If it does not affect authorization, it is ordinary / internal training and is not TOT’s procedure-training scope.

  • General technical training
  • Internal development training
  • Non-authorization related courses

Need a working register of: customer, training, assigned person, and status (planned / in progress / complete / in LMS / QA verified).

2. Who owns the customer — three branches

Homebase — TOT proactive

CX  |  UO (HK Express)  |  HKA  |  HKC (Hong Kong Cargo)

QT Jason / TOT proactively coordinates training requirements for customers with a significant Hong Kong line-maintenance presence.

  • TOT initiates and follows through
  • Still needs records into the shared folder and internal LMS
Other 3rd party — Operations owns

ANA and other non-home-base operators

The respective Operations team manages required customer training and records. TOT does not proactively pull the customer system or backfill LMS.

  • Highest current gap (see section 6)
  • Ops must file records, then ask TOT to coordinate LMS
UA exception — TOT Jack / MIST

United Airlines only

TOT Jack pulls CT reports from the UA MIST system. MIST has about 20 requirements to keep the licence usable. QA is concerned with about 5 that affect authorization.

  • Those 5 need training evidence
  • TOT places evidence in the internal LMS
  • QA checks LMS — not customer systems one-by-one
  • This support is UA-only

4. Three training delivery types

A. Customer instructor

Customer trainer flies in (or delivers on site) and trains HAECO staff. Attendance and completion evidence still must reach the folder and LMS.

B. Internal HAECO trainer — highest gap

Qualified HAECO trainer delivers the course. This is where records are most often missing: trained, but no file, so LMS is not updated and QA treats it as not trained.

C. Computer-based training (CBT)

Online / e-learning / web-based modules or customer learning portals. Completion evidence must still be extracted and filed internally.

5. Priority gap — trained but not authorized

The problem is not that training is skipped. The break is after delivery, especially for non-homebase / non-TOT-owned customer training, and especially when an internal trainer delivers the course.

Training completed → No record captured → Not submitted to TOT → LMS not updated → QA cannot verify → Authorization rejected

Fix first: Operations files the record (correct folder + email, or email the files) so TOT can submit a TA-accepted request and TA can update the internal LMS before QA reviews authorization.

6. CMP future path — competence personal record

CMP (Competency Management Project). OCG / GoGo supports authorization data, customer training visibility, competency tracking, and authorization compliance monitoring.

Today

Soft copies

Certificates, attendance sheets, training records and authorization evidence sit in the shared folder as files.

Digitize

Readable data

Convert documents into structured, searchable fields / codes that a system can read.

Control

QA approve

QA approves the data structure and the evidence before it is used for competency decisions.

Then CMP judges

Competency view

Courses completed, qualifications held, customer approvals, authorization eligibility, and training gaps.

7. Roles and unresolved ownership

Role Does Does not / open point
TOT (Jason / Jack; coordinator) Authorization-impacting customer procedure training. Proactive for CX, UO, HKA, HKC. UA MIST CT reports (Jack). Maintains the shared folder for now. Formats and submits LMS requests to TA. Does not own the LMS. Does not proactively run other third-party customers (e.g. ANA).
Operations Owns other third-party customer training and records. Delivers or hosts training. Files completed records and notifies TOT. If records stay on a desk or in email, LMS and authorization fail even when the person was trained.
Training Academy (TA) System owner of the internal LMS. Accepts a correctly formatted request, then updates the system. Does not invent missing attendance; TOT / Ops must submit complete evidence.
QA Verifies license, type course, authorization training and customer procedure evidence from internal records. Approves or rejects authorization. Audits compliance. QA’s stated position is audit / governance, not custody of all training records. Who is the long-term record owner among QA, Ops, TOT and TA is still open.
OCG / GoGo (CMP) Support CMP: authorization data, customer training visibility, competency tracking, compliance monitoring. Depends on the current record loop being closed first; CMP cannot judge what was never filed.
