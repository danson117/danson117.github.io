How the two lists were combined

Green rows are EK adds. A green name in EK GOM name is a GOM title kept on the matching base row.

  • Human Factors Training sits on the professional-integrity row. Understanding of Human Factors Principles sits on the human-performance row. Same course, same LMS certificate, source CP+GOM.
  • Safe Use of Tools and Equipment, Compliance with Safety Procedures and PPE Requirements, and Communication of Operational and Safety Information stay on the tooling, PPE, and communication rows. The GOM extract tagged those three titles GOM only; the base map already says CP+GOM, so Source stays CP+GOM.
  • FOD Awareness and Prevention stays on the final-clearance row (LM00082, LMS certificate). Source stays CP+GOM. The shorter GOM-only list had tagged FOD as GOM; the row map and the detailed extract both say CP+GOM.
  • EWIS Awareness (where applicable) stays on the T.C774 row. Source is CP+GOM+Regulator. Enhanced EWIS (T.C775) and Fuel Tank Safety (T.EE0131) stay Regulator, as in the row map.
  • Dangerous Goods Awareness (LMS certificate, GOM) is separate from Ability to process removed, uninstalled and rejected parts (same course, OJT record, CP+GOM).
  • Participation in Recurrent Training Programme is the programme-level GOM line (HF / EWIS / FTS). It does not replace Recurrent Human Factors or Company Procedures Refresher.
  • Training Records Must Remain Current and Available is a GOM add. It is not one of the five priority items.

Priority five: 1 Dangerous Goods Awareness, 2 DG incident recognition and reporting, 3 SMS awareness plus safety-event reporting, 4 FOD prevention, 5 recurrent training programme.
