Minimum fields required for this dashboard

Each row must record: current step, blocker, client requirements, internal QA requirements, target date, estimated days remaining, trainer source, session duration, earliest available slot, and the owner of the next action.

Without these fields, the view is merely a name list. With them, it is possible to identify who requires escalation and who may be scheduled.

How this process should be run

The requirement is not additional spreadsheets. It is one team progress register: current step, point of delay, outstanding client versus internal QA items, and trainer availability.

The process is fixed at seven steps: 1 Requirements collection; 2 Client-side; 3 Internal QA; 4 Trainer and session scheduling; 5 Delivery; 6 Examination and authorization; 7 Completion and filing. Client requirements and internal QA must be maintained as separate columns.

Week 1: agree the process and fields. Week 2: update step, blocker, and next-action owner each Monday. Week 3: load the live roster into this dashboard. Standardize the process first, complete the fields second, and automate last.
