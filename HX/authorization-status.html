Weekly checklist (takeover)

Use this every week. Do not treat it as a second problem register.

  • Review customer rosters and LMS match.
  • List authorizations expiring within 90 days.
  • Follow pending customer approvals and training.
  • Check coverage versus minimum by customer and fleet.
  • Close open findings and overdue acknowledgements.
  • Remove resigned or transferred staff from the active list.
  • Brief duty and front-line supervisors when a validity or type issue arises.

Files to obtain first

Ask for these on day one. They feed the tables below.

  • Current customer roster by airline.
  • Expiry list for the next 12 months.
  • Pending applications.
  • Suspended / restricted list.
  • Read-and-sign tracker.
  • Minimum certifier coverage matrix by customer and type.
