This trial pulls UAL CBT and CT Reminder into one board. OCG / CG / PAX tabs and Other / Team 1–4 columns use the current Name List. LTW1–4 count as Team 1–4. Overdue or will expire (red) combines CT overdue, CT coming due, and UAL ≤30 days. Coming due (yellow) is UAL >30 days only. Click a row for course details (this browser only).
