    Crew List (Pax Teams 1–4) joined to OCS staff extract. Updated 20 Sep 2026.

    Full List shows every staff (OCG). Name List columns come first (CG/PAX, Staff No, Staff Name, Chinese Name, Home Section, Current Section, Staff Position, Position Title, Class, Status, Date join, Date Term, Revenue Code), then Crew List columns that are not duplicates (Team, Shift, Ω, UA, MM Refueller, JL, JJP, Remark).

    OCG = CG + PAX (the total). A person is CG or PAX only. PAX = Crew List, including FLS and RF. CG = Name List, not on Crew List. Certifiers = Licensed Engineer / Assistant Licensed Engineer / Controller / Duty in Charge (including Deputy DIC and OCG PAX / CARGO / UPS DIC). Office = Manager / Assistant Manager / support / liaison / project officer / operations officer — not Controller, not Duty in Charge. Mechanics = AMM, technician, refueller, and Supervisor. Click a counter to filter. Click a header to sort. Drag a header to change column order.
